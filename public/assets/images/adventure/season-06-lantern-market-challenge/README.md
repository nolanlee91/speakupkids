# Season 06 — The Lantern Market Challenge

Independent six-chapter Adventure season for learners around ages 10–12 (A2+ to light B1).

## Core journey

1. Finish the market plan.
2. Place stalls while protecting access and food safety.
3. Read ingredient information and respond to an allergy requirement.
4. Compare supply bundles and keep a budget reserve.
5. Match deliveries to destination symbols and priorities.
6. Open the market with a clear, useful public message.

The season is independent from Learn and Practice. Progress, chapter rewards and unlock order are defined in `lib/adventures.ts`.

## Asset structure

- `map/season-06-lantern-market-map.webp`: background only; React renders routes, nodes and Maple.
- `chapters/`: six 4:3 chapter illustrations.
- `items/`: nine transparent collectible WebP files plus a reference sticker sheet.

## Visual continuity rule

- Maple is the **only anthropomorphic beaver**.
- Supporting characters are stylized 3D humans with varied ages and backgrounds.
- Never turn Maple into a human or give human supporting characters fur, muzzles or tails.
- Keep the premium cinematic 3D storybook look used by Seasons 4–6.
- Avoid repeating the same helper, counter composition or pavilion setting across chapters.

## Content rule

Questions must use details clearly visible in the matching chapter image. Do not treat decorative/generated image text as evidence; use pictograms, physical objects, placement, sequence and dialogue from the TypeScript data.
