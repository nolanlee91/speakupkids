# Season 08 — Mission Moonbase

Independent six-chapter Adventure season for learners around ages 10–12 (A2+ to light B1).

## Core journey

1. Assign four crew roles from skill evidence.
2. Prioritize essential cargo under a capacity limit.
3. Follow a repeatable airlock safety sequence.
4. Compare terrain, sunlight and energy to choose a rover route.
5. Write a complete update for delayed communication.
6. Balance water, light, energy, terrain and access to select a greenhouse site.

The season is independent from Learn and Practice. Progress, chapter rewards and unlock order are defined in `lib/adventures.ts`.

## Asset structure

- `map/season-08-moonbase-map.webp`: clean background only; React renders routes, nodes and Maple.
- `chapters/`: six 4:3 chapter illustrations.
- `items/`: nine transparent collectible WebP files plus a reference sticker sheet.

## Visual continuity rule

- Maple is the **only anthropomorphic beaver**.
- Maple wears a fitted teal-and-white lunar suit designed around her animal proportions and broad tail.
- Keep her brown fur, rounded muzzle, ears, auburn ponytail and maple-leaf clip visible whenever practical.
- Supporting crew are stylized 3D humans; never give them fur, muzzles or tails.
- Keep the premium cinematic 3D storybook look used by Seasons 4–8.
- Maps must not contain baked-in pins, node numbers, dotted UI routes or labels.

## Content rule

- Questions must rely on clearly visible pictograms, objects, sequences, terrain and indicator bars.
- Do not treat decorative or generated screen text as evidence.
- Keep the science near-future and internally plausible; the goal is reasoning and English communication, not fantasy technology.
