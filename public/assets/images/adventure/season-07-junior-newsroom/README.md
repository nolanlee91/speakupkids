# Season 07 — The Junior Newsroom: Fact or Rumour?

Independent six-chapter Adventure season for learners around ages 10–12 (A2+ to light B1).

## Core journey

1. Separate a widely shared claim from verified information.
2. Compare a cropped photograph with its full context.
3. Record two witness accounts and their viewpoint limits.
4. check a primary notice for source, scope and dates.
5. Rewrite a sensational or vague headline using confirmed facts.
6. Present a transparent evidence-based correction.

The season is independent from Learn and Practice. Progress, chapter rewards and unlock order are defined in `lib/adventures.ts`.

## Asset structure

- `map/season-07-newsroom-city-map.webp`: clean background only; React renders routes, nodes and Maple.
- `chapters/`: six 4:3 chapter illustrations.
- `items/`: nine transparent collectible WebP files plus a reference sticker sheet.

## Visual continuity rule

- Maple is the **only anthropomorphic beaver**.
- Supporting characters are stylized 3D humans with varied ages and backgrounds.
- Never give human supporting characters fur, muzzles or tails.
- Keep the premium cinematic 3D storybook look used by Seasons 4–7.
- Use a different environment and composition for each reporting stage.
- Maps must not contain baked-in pins, node numbers, dotted routes or UI.

## Content rule

- Do not treat decorative or generated screen text as evidence.
- Questions must rely on visible pictograms, framing, physical objects, viewpoints and the dialogue in `lib/adventures.ts`.
- Present media literacy as a repeatable process: pause, identify the source, restore context, compare accounts and verify with a primary source.
